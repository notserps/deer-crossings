Source recording: **“Deer Meewing 01”** by **msantoro11 (Freesound)**, available through Pixabay.

Source:
`https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/nature-deer-meewing-01-31634/`

Pixabay lists the recording as free for use under the **Pixabay Content License**. The release uses only short edited clips as part of the mod rather than distributing the source recording as a standalone sound library.
